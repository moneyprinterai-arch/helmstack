2. Background

  • The Company is undertaking a seed financing round to accelerate product development, scale distribution, and establish international growth (Seed Round).
  • The Company intends to complete a share split (subdivision) prior to Completion so that the Company will have 1,000,000 ordinary shares on issue pre-investment (Share Split).
  • Following the Share Split, the Investor wishes to subscribe for ordinary shares. The Investor may choose the number of shares they wish to subscribe for, and the cash Subscription Amount is calculated as: Subscription Shares × Subscription Price per share.

3. Definitions & Interpretation

Business DayA day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Auckland, New Zealand.

Share SplitThe subdivision of the Company's existing 100 ordinary shares into 1,000,000 ordinary shares (each existing share becomes 10,000 shares) without changing ownership percentages.
Subscription PriceThe price per ordinary share, being AUD 25.00 (unless otherwise agreed in Schedule 1).

References to shares are to ordinary shares of the Company, post Share Split unless stated otherwise.

4. Subscription

4.1 Subscription. Subject to clause 5, the Investor subscribes for the Subscription Shares and the Company agrees to issue the Subscription Shares on Completion.

4.2 Subscription Price. The Subscription Price is AUD 25.00 per share (indicative), unless otherwise agreed in Schedule 1.

4.3 Subscription Amount. The Subscription Amount is calculated as: Subscription Shares × Subscription Price, and is payable in cleared funds in accordance with clause 6.

4.4 Ranking. The Subscription Shares will rank equally with all other ordinary shares on issue (post Share Split).

5. Conditions Precedent

  • Share Split: the Share Split has been validly approved and completed, and the share register updated to reflect 1,000,000 ordinary shares on issue pre-investment.
  • Approvals: all necessary corporate approvals and resolutions required for the issue and allotment of the Subscription Shares have been obtained.
  • KYC/AML: the Investor provides customary KYC/AML and source-of-funds information reasonably required.
  • Definitive investor rights: entry into any agreed shareholders' agreement / investor rights deed / constitution (if applicable).
  • No illegality: the Subscription is not prohibited by applicable law in any relevant jurisdiction.

6. Completion

6.1 Completion Date. Completion will occur on the date agreed in writing between the parties after the conditions in clause 5 are satisfied or waived (where permitted).

6.2 Payment. The Investor must pay the Subscription Amount in cleared funds to the Company's nominated bank account:

  • Investment decision: the Investor subscribes as principal for its own account and has made its own independent evaluation.
  • Risk acknowledgement: early-stage investments are speculative and may result in total loss.

  • Compliance: the Investor will comply with applicable laws (including securities laws) in its jurisdiction.

8. Company Representations & Warranties

  • Valid issue: upon receipt of the Subscription Amount, the Subscription Shares will be validly issued and rank equally with other ordinary shares.
